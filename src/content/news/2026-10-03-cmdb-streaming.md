We are working on a **configuration management data streaming platform**.

How can you secure your infrastructure if you do not know the shape and scope of your
infrastructure at any given moment?

Without that picture, every change to infrastructure, applications, and their configuration
is made blind. You cannot see the relationships and dependencies between components, so you
cannot know what a change will affect until something breaks.
