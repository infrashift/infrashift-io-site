---
title: Migrating from GitHub to SSHadowForge
date: 2026-10-03
---

We will soon migrate our repositories from GitHub to **SSHadowForge**, our own forge.
GitHub will remain a mirror for public releases of our solutions.

Why? We want hermetic, reproducible builds, including the ability to re-run historic builds
on historically accurate infrastructure.

This might seem extreme. But if we can achieve this level of control, we can live up to our
core tenet: **"Don't Trust Us, Verify Us"**.
